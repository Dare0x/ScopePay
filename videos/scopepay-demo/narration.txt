Most online work still ends with a message that says: trust me.
The file was delivered. The payment was sent. The proof is somewhere in a chat thread.
ScopePay makes the important moments visible, before the money moves.

Start with the exact scope.
A client funds a committed deal in USDC on Arbitrum. The total is visible. The milestones are named. The terms are locked before the work starts.
That small change matters. It gives both sides one shared starting point, instead of two different memories of the promise.

Then the worker delivers.
Each milestone carries evidence: a working result, a commitment, and a time-stamped record. The client can see what changed, what is owed, and what can be released.
No vague handoff. No disappearing attachment. The result stays attached to the decision.

Approve a milestone and the balance moves with the approval.
The activity record keeps the receipt next to the payment, so a completed task becomes portable work history.

And when work disagrees, ScopePay does not pretend the disagreement is not there.
The disputed milestone freezes. The rest of the deal stays legible. A named arbiter reviews the evidence and resolves only the current milestone.
The contract records the outcome without rewriting what happened before it.

Every result stays checkable: the contract, the deal, the transaction, and the worker record.
That is the difference between a payment notification and a settlement you can prove.

ScopePay turns internet work into a clear sequence.
Fund the scope.
Deliver the work.
Approve the evidence.
Keep the receipt.
