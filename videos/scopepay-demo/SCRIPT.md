# ScopePay — 90-second narration

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:08)

Work should be settled by what was delivered, not by who has more leverage.

## Scene 2 — Scope secured (0:08–0:22)

ScopePay is milestone escrow for independent work. A client funds the scope, a worker delivers evidence, and payment follows approval.

## Scene 3 — Evidence to payment (0:22–0:42)

The first milestone is submitted, approved, and released directly from the contract. The payment and the activity record are visible to both sides.

## Scene 4 — Dispute path (0:42–1:05)

If the parties disagree, the escrow freezes. The named arbiter reviews the current milestone and records the settlement split onchain.

## Scene 5 — Public receipt (1:05–1:20)

Every state change has a public receipt on Arbitrum Sepolia. The contract, the deal, and the settlement can be checked independently.

## Scene 6 — Close (1:20–1:30)

ScopePay turns internet work into a payment rail with evidence attached. Work delivered. Payment earned. Proof retained.
